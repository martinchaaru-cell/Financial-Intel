Independent pay intelligence for Kenyan boards

We turn the governance and remuneration disclosures in listed companies' annual reports into clean, comparable benchmarks.

Why we exist

Board pay decisions deserve better evidence

Every year, nomination and remuneration committees decide what to pay their chair, non-executive directors and chief executive. The information they need is already public, spread across dozens of annual reports in different formats, units and levels of detail.

Financial Intel collects those reports, reads the governance and remuneration sections, puts every figure on the same basis and checks it against the page it came from. The result is a benchmark a board can rely on and a company secretary can cite.

Sourced, not surveyed

Our figures come from what companies publish, not from questionnaires, so every number can be traced and checked.

Comparable

Pay reported per year, per month or in thousands is converted to the same units, and other currencies to Kenya shillings at the year's average rate.

Independent

We don't sell pay advice or board placements. We provide the evidence; your committee makes the call.
