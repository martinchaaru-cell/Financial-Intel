Questions

Where do the figures come from?

From each company's published annual or integrated report. Every figure is linked to the page it came from and checked before it is included.

How do I pay?

By card or M-Pesa from your Billing page, or by bank transfer against an invoice. Invoices carry our KRA PIN and can be issued through eTIMS.

What happens when my trial ends?

Pay the invoice to continue. If you don't, your account returns to a free account; nothing is charged automatically during a trial.

Can several colleagues share one account?

Each person gets their own sign-in, and each account can be signed in on one device at a time. Team and Enterprise plans include several users.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and nothing more is charged.
